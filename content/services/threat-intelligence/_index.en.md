Threat intelligence is the collection and assessment of information about attackers, their methods and their infrastructure. Threat hunting is the targeted search of IT systems for signs of an undetected compromise; detection rules are derived from it (detection engineering).

## Typical questions

- Which threat actors and attack methods are relevant for an organisation?
- Are there signs of a compromise that has not yet been detected?
- Which detection rules are missing from existing monitoring?
