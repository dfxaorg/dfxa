An IT forensic expert opinion is a written, reproducible assessment of digital traces by an expert. It answers specific questions put by a court, an authority or a party, based on preserved data and documented methods.

## Typical questions

- Which data are stored on a storage device, a mobile phone or in a user account, and when were they created, modified or deleted?
- Can a specific action be attributed to a person, an account or a device?
- Is the digital evidence presented complete and unaltered?
