Decentralised finance (DeFi) applications consist of smart contracts, i.e. programs executed on a blockchain. The analysis establishes how a protocol works and how specific transactions unfolded, for example in an exploit or a case of market manipulation.

## Typical questions

- How did an attack on a DeFi protocol unfold technically?
- What role did a specific smart contract play in a transaction?
- Where did funds flow via decentralised exchanges, bridges or mixers?
