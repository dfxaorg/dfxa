Digital Forensics Austria – Network of Independent Experts (DFXA) is an association of independent digital forensics experts in Austria. This website presents the experts and their fields and helps visitors find the relevant expert for a specific question.

## How the network works

The network is not a company. It does not accept engagements, issue offers or invoice. Each expert in the network acts exclusively in their own name and for their own account, and decides independently whether to accept an engagement.

The shared contact address serves only to forward enquiries to the experts. An enquiry is answered by the relevant expert.

## Independence

The experts in the network are independent of one another. Their cooperation within the network is limited to the shared website and the shared contact address. Before accepting an engagement, each expert checks independently whether there are grounds for bias or conflicts of interest.

## Admission criteria

An expert can be admitted to the network if they

- are registered in the Austrian list of court experts as a sworn and court-certified expert witness in a field of digital forensics, and
- obtain the consent of all experts in the network.
