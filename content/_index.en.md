Digital Forensics Austria (DFXA) is a network of independent digital forensics experts in Austria. This website presents the experts in the network and their fields – from IT forensics and IT security to incident response, malware analysis and cryptoasset forensics. Enquiries are addressed to the relevant expert, who accepts engagements in their own name.
