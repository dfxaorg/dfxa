This privacy policy explains which personal data are processed when visiting the website dfxa.at and when sending enquiries by email.

## No cookies, no analytics, no contact form

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking services, does not embed external fonts, scripts, maps, videos or social media elements, and does not offer a contact form.

## Hosting

The website is served via Cloudflare Workers. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare operates a worldwide network of data centres for this purpose, including within the EU. TODO: legal review – check Cloudflare's address, contracting entity and Data Processing Addendum.

When the website is accessed, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data, in particular the IP address, date and time of access, the page requested and information about the browser used. The processing serves to provide the website and keep it secure, for example to defend against attacks. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare features that set cookies or collect analytics data are disabled for this website.

This may involve a transfer of data to the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to the extent that Cloudflare, Inc. is certified under it. ## Enquiries by email to the contact address

### Joint controllership

For enquiries to {{< contact-email >}}, the experts of the network are joint controllers under Art. 26 GDPR:

- Priv.-Doz. DI Mag. Dr. Bernhard Haslhofer, Metternichgasse 8, 1030 Vienna, Austria
- DI Johann Stockinger, Metternichgasse 8, 1030 Vienna, Austria

The essence of the joint controllership arrangement (Art. 26(2) GDPR):

- {{< contact-email >}} is a forwarding address. Each enquiry is delivered to both experts' own mailboxes; there is no shared mailbox.
- Within two working days, the experts agree who answers the enquiry. From then on, that expert is the sole controller for any further processing.
- The other expert deletes their copy of the enquiry within 30 days unless it is needed for a conflict-of-interest check.
- DI Johann Stockinger maintains this privacy policy and is the contact for notifications of personal data breaches.
- Data subjects can exercise their rights against either expert.

### Data processed, purpose and legal basis

The email address, the name and the information provided in the enquiry are processed. The purpose is to answer the enquiry and, where applicable, to prepare an engagement. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or the legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Forwarding

Mail to {{< contact-email >}} is forwarded to the experts' mailboxes via Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., address see above). Cloudflare processes the message, including the sender address and content, in order to deliver it; this may involve a transfer of data to the USA (on the same basis as for hosting). ## Emails to an expert's own address

For emails sent directly to an expert's own email address, that expert is the sole controller.

## Retention

Data from enquiries are deleted once they are no longer needed for answering the enquiry or for any statutory retention obligations.

## Data subject rights

Data subjects have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). Requests can be sent to {{< contact-email >}} or directly to either expert.
